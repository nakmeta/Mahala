About this piece

The silk
Golden silk from Cambodian mulberry silkworms, a strain that nearly disappeared in the 1970s and is now farmed again in Takeo and Banteay Meanchey.
The weave
Plain hand-weave on a treadle loom, finished with a hand-knotted fringe. Small irregularities in the weave are normal and are the mark of a handmade cloth.
The artisan
Made by Srey Mom, who has woven silk for 22 years and now trains six younger weavers in her village workshop.
Caring for it
Hand wash in cool water with mild soap, then dry in the shade. Never wring the cloth, and never use bleach.
