Frequently asked

How long does delivery take?
One to two days inside Phnom Penh, and two to four days to other provinces. We send a Telegram message with the tracking number as soon as the parcel leaves the shop.
How much is delivery?
A flat $3 anywhere in Cambodia, and free on any order over $50.
How can I pay?
ABA bank transfer, Wing, or cash to the driver when the parcel arrives.
Can I return something?
Yes — within 14 days, as long as the item is unused and still has its tag. Food and spices cannot be returned once opened.

Still not sure?

Send us a message using the form above, or call the shop directly during opening hours. We speak Khmer and English.
